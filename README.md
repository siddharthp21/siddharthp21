# 💫 About Me:
🚀 About Me<br>I am an Embedded Systems & IoT Developer and an Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering student at Pravara Rural Engineering College (Class of 2026). My work focuses on building "intelligence" into hardware, specializing in firmware development and real-time system design.Currently, I am developing a Context-Aware Audio Guidance System—a pair of AI-powered smart glasses designed to provide real-time spatial awareness for the visually impaired using Raspberry Pi and Computer Vision. I thrive at the intersection of C/C++, Python, and hardware abstraction.<br><br>🛠 Tech Stack & Expertise<br>· Core: Embedded C, Python, C++, Verilog, Data Structures.<br>· Hardware: STM32, Raspberry Pi, ESP32, Arduino, TI TM4C123G.<br>· Protocols & Tools: UART, I2C, SPI, RTOS fundamentals, STM32CubeIDE, Keil uVision.<br>· Interests: Edge AI, Robotics, and IoT-based safety solutions.<br><br>🌟 Beyond the Code<br>· Research: Presented my work on IoT-based Accident Detection Systems at the PRECCON 2025 National Conference.<br>· Languages: Multilingual in English, Hindi, Marathi, and currently mastering Japanese (N5).<br>· Community: Active member of the Pravara Association of Communication Engineering (PACE) and a volunteer at the Shree Ram Foundation.<br><br>I’m always open to discussing firmware architecture, IoT security, or potential project collaborations.
